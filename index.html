Import accounts by cookie
Paste one .ROBLOSECURITY cookie per line. Each valid cookie becomes an account (username, avatar, etc. are filled automatically).
  1. Install the Cookie-Editor browser extension (Chrome / Firefox / Edge).
  2. Go to roblox.com and log into the account.
  3. Open Cookie-Editor → find .ROBLOSECURITY → copy its value.
  4. Paste it below (one cookie per line if importing many).
Beta — use at your own risk. This website is in beta. We are not responsible for any leaked, stolen, lost, or banned accounts. Only import cookies you own and trust this environment.

Password 🔑
Collab only. Password tools unlock when your account has a collab plan. Buy under Collab, pay via Discord ticket, then staff activate it on your username.
Changing a Roblox password rotates its .ROBLOSECURITY cookie — ogalts captures the new cookie automatically, so accounts stay logged in. No login or captcha needed.
Auto-change on generateRight after a new account is generated, immediately change its password to one you control, and keep the rotated cookie.
New password formatEvery generated password starts with your prefix, then random letters and digits, padded to the total length. Used by both auto-change and the per-account change button in Accounts.
